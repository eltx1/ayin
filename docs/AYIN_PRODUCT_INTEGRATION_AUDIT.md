## Workflow baseline (source-derived, runtime measurement pending)

- Viewer navigation: Home/Search/My AYIN and flagged catalog destinations are directly linked; four catalog links currently terminate in placeholders, so low click count alone is not success.
- Creator: dashboard to quick upload is intended to be one navigation action. Existing `/channel/*` and `/studio/*` wrappers share components; these are not evidence of duplicated business logic.
- Admin: each listed area is one sidebar click but 19 undifferentiated choices create scanning cost. Operations contains dashboard, staffing, support, compliance and audit in one long workspace.
- Watch: preserve player/ad/progress and next-content context. Upload: retain direct-to-R2 and automatic processing, progressively disclose advanced metadata.
